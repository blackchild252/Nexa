Items begin NOT_STARTED; a status changes only when its own evidence is recorded. Block 01 may carry audit-baseline statuses while feature evidence remains open; prior V1 completion labels do not transfer automatically. Carry forward unresolved V1 gaps explicitly. External credentials, live providers, production deployment, devices, stores, and independent reviews remain NOT TESTED/DEFERRED until authorized and evidenced.

## Transition and carry-forward rules

- V1 is superseded, not erased; its historic Marketplace work and audit evidence remain preserved.
- The prior 75 MISFIT_OPEN assignments and 425 items without item-level evidence audit are inherited obligations. V2 Block 01 must reconcile each item to evidence, a new V2 item, or a documented closure decision.
- Block-level theme fit is not item-level completion. A status can change only with a concrete source/test/evidence reference.
- Main owns consumer, vendor, payments, Dami and moderation canonical state. Admin uses only its own operational authority and read-only sanitized Main evidence where applicable.
- Do not call NEXA globally ready until the final block’s acceptance criteria are evidenced and all external gates are explicitly resolved or formally excluded by the owner.

- `AUDIT_BASELINE_COMPLETE_CARRY_FORWARD_OPEN` means the baseline inventory is evidenced, not that the underlying features or all legacy target mappings are complete.
- `AUDIT_COMPLETE_CARRY_FORWARD_OPEN` means the audit/documentation task has evidence; feature obligations may remain open and must stay listed in the carry-forward ledger.
- `CANDIDATE_TARGETS_ASSIGNED_VALIDATION_OPEN` means a V2 target is assigned provisionally and must be semantically checked before the historical misfit is closed.
