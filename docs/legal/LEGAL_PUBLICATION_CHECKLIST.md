# NEXA Legal Publication Checklist

This checklist is a release dependency. Draft documents are not published legal advice.

- [ ] Replace privacy contact placeholder.
- [ ] Replace legal/Terms contact placeholder.
- [ ] Confirm NEXA legal entity and address.
- [ ] Confirm minimum-age policy and child-audience classification.
- [ ] Confirm all processors/service providers and their purposes.
- [ ] Confirm payment provider disclosures.
- [ ] Confirm AI provider disclosures and applicable processing terms.
- [ ] Confirm AdMob/Google disclosures and consent configuration.
- [ ] Confirm analytics/crash-reporting disclosures.
- [ ] Confirm data-transfer language and applicable mechanisms.
- [ ] Confirm retention periods against the production database policy.
- [ ] Confirm deletion exceptions for financial/security/legal records.
- [ ] Publish Privacy Policy over HTTPS.
- [ ] Publish Terms over HTTPS.
- [ ] Set `EXPO_PUBLIC_PRIVACY_POLICY_URL` to the published Privacy Policy URL.
- [ ] Set `EXPO_PUBLIC_TERMS_URL` to the published Terms URL.
- [ ] Verify both URLs from a production build.
- [ ] Complete Google Play Data Safety / Data deletion declarations.
- [ ] Complete Apple privacy disclosures.
- [ ] Complete legal review before store submission.
