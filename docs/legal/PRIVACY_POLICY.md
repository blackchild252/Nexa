# NEXA Privacy Policy — Draft for Legal Review

**Policy version:** 2026-10-01.v3  
**Status:** Technical product draft — publish only after business/legal review.

## Information we collect
NEXA may process account information such as email, name, username, profile information, privacy choices, authentication/session records, notifications, user-generated content, marketplace/vendor activity, communications, AI feature data, preferences, device/app information, and security/audit information needed to operate and protect the service. NEXA may also process information generated when users use camera, microphone, image upload, calls, notifications, payments, or other features they choose to use.

## How we use information
We use information to provide NEXA features, authenticate accounts, protect accounts, operate communications and marketplace features, process user-requested AI workflows, provide customer support, prevent abuse and fraud, maintain service security, diagnose failures, meet legal obligations, and operate advertising where enabled.

## Advertising
Yaayz may display Google AdMob advertising. Advertising and consent behavior can depend on the user's location, device settings, applicable law, and choices presented through Google's consent tooling. NEXA does not treat an advertising consent choice as permission to use unrelated NEXA account data for unrelated purposes.

## AI
Some NEXA features use AI providers. Information sent to an AI provider is limited by the applicable feature and NEXA's technical privacy controls. NEXA should not be represented as sending every account field to every AI provider. Users should avoid submitting information they are not authorized to share.

## Permissions
Depending on features used, NEXA may request camera, microphone, photo-library, notification, and advertising/tracking permissions. Permissions are requested for the corresponding feature and can be changed through device settings where supported.

## Sharing
NEXA may share information with service providers and infrastructure partners required to provide requested functionality, including authentication, hosting, payments, communications, AI, error monitoring, analytics, and advertising. Provider-specific processing should be described in the final published policy and store disclosures.

## Retention
NEXA applies feature-specific retention and deletion rules. Some transaction, accounting, fraud-prevention, security, and legal records may need to remain after account closure. Other account-associated information is eligible for deletion through the account lifecycle process.

## Account deletion
Users can initiate account closure from the NEXA account settings flow. Closure revokes active sessions and performs the configured account cleanup. Records that NEXA must retain for transactional, legal, security, or integrity purposes may be retained and appropriately redacted or anonymized.

## Data export
NEXA provides an account export flow for eligible portable account information. Credentials, sessions, provider payment records, vendor credentials, push tokens, and system/admin information are excluded from the portable export boundary.

## Security
NEXA uses authentication controls, authorization boundaries, session revocation, sensitive-field redaction, account ownership checks, integrity checks, rate limiting, and operational monitoring. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

## Age requirement and children
NEXA is intended only for people aged 18 years and older. Children and anyone under 18 are not permitted to create or use a NEXA account. NEXA does not intentionally offer the service to children. Signup requires an explicit 18+ confirmation, including before Google account sign-in can create or access a NEXA session. If NEXA learns that an account is being used by someone under 18, NEXA may restrict or close the account and handle associated information according to its retention and legal obligations.

## International data processing and transfers
NEXA and its service providers may process, store, or transfer information in countries other than the user's country. This may occur through hosting, authentication, payments, communications, AI, analytics, error monitoring, advertising, backups, or other infrastructure needed to provide NEXA. NEXA uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards and, where required by applicable law, appropriate contractual, adequacy-based, or other legally recognized transfer mechanisms. The specific providers and applicable safeguards should be identified in the final published privacy disclosures when required.

## Your choices and rights
Depending on applicable law, users may have rights concerning access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, objection, consent withdrawal, and complaints. NEXA should provide the applicable request/contact channel in the published policy.
