## Closure rules

1. `MISFIT_TARGET_VALIDATED_FEATURE_OPEN` means the V2 target and domain were validated for a historical misfit. The historical execution range remains misassigned in V1 and the feature obligation remains open; do not rewrite V1 history.
2. `EVIDENCE_AUDIT_REQUIRED` means the prior row has not received an individual implementation evidence audit. Do not change it to implemented without concrete source and test evidence.
3. Every V2 feature item remains `NOT_STARTED` unless its own source/test evidence is independently recorded in the V2 register and execution roadmap. Target validation is not feature completion.
4. External provider, credentials, live/deployed acceptance, legal review and physical-device checks stay `DEFERRED`/`NOT TESTED` until genuinely verified.
5. Historical Marketplace execution work remains intact and cannot be counted as completion of International Commerce, Customer Care, or Trust/Safety.
