## Audit rules
- `IMPLEMENTED_LOCAL` means the source implementation and local contract evidence were found; it does not mean live acceptance or global readiness is certified.
- `PARTIAL` means only a subset/foundation was evidenced or an important end-to-end property remains unverified.
- `DEFERRED_EXTERNAL` means credentials/provider/legal/device/live acceptance is outside this local run.
- `PARTIAL_INTEGRATION` means Admin delegates to Main-owned endpoints and its own wrappers/UI were inspected/tested; it is not independent Admin auth authority.
- `TARGET_REVIEW_REQUIRED` identifies candidate V2 target wording that does not semantically match the legacy requirement closely enough to treat the candidate as validated.
- No item is automatically certified complete because its candidate target exists. Main and Admin evidence remains separate.

## Findings and next gates
- The authentication/recovery/session foundation is substantially present in local source and covered by local contract tests.
- Email-provider delivery, deployed cross-app acceptance, production database/API behavior, browser/device flows, and external provider acceptance were not tested.
- Durable account lock state and complete chronological login-history UX are not certified by this slice.
- Candidate-target mismatches remain explicitly open for semantic review; this audit does not alter the locked V1 register/crosswalk or transfer historical completion.
- Country-specific document policies remain reference-only with `manual_policy_review_required`; legal review and provider integration are deferred.
- All changes and tests are local; no production credentials, EAS, app-store, or launch actions were used.
